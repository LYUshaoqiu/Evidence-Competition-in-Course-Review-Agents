## Interpretation boundary

This is a clean-set descriptive baseline, not an attack result and not a statistical test. The cohort includes user-approved annotation exceptions; all summary statistics exclude incomplete review annotations, while raw review collections remain available to later agents. It establishes the fixed gold set and the evidence-landscape measurements that every contaminated condition must use. No claim about dilution, detector bypass, or agent vulnerability is licensed until the preregistered synthetic conditions and agent outputs have been run.
